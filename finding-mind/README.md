# Finding Mind — website

Landing page for **Finding Mind**, L.G. Meredith's book on the origin of Mind as a
recapitulation of the origin of Life.

Single self-contained HTML file — no build step, no external assets except Google Fonts.

## Structure

```
index.html      The whole site. Inline <style>, inline SVG, inline <script>.
_src/gen.py     Generates the roundel SVG that is pasted into index.html.
```

## The window

The hero is a stained-glass rose window built as inline SVG (~950 panes), generated by
`_src/gen.py` rather than drawn by hand. It is the argument, not decoration:

- **Three rings, two thresholds** — matter (cold mineral shards), life (cellular forms),
  mind (radiating light). The two thresholds are the two unexplained transitions in the
  geologic record that the book claims are the same event.
- **The leading is the lattice.** Panes are irreducible; the lead that holds them is a
  graph. Nothing lights until something passes through it.
- **Gametes** in the life ring are paired forms meeting at a lead node and exchanging what
  they carry — the reduction rule of a reflective calculus drawn as what it already is.
- **The serpent** is dark glass, coiling inward on a spiral and taking its own tail:
  reflection, `@P` quoted into a name and `*x` dereferenced back into a process.
- **Fireflies** in the matter ring are weakly coupled oscillators (Kuramoto). They start
  out of phase and pull each other into sync over ~30s. Nobody is in charge.

To redraw every pane, change the seed at the top of `_src/gen.py`, run it, and paste the
new `window.svg` into `index.html` in place of the existing `<svg id="window">…</svg>`.

## Type

EB Garamond stands in for **Minion**, which Greg chose (lightest weight, June 2025) and
which is not web-licensed here. Swap to Minion via Adobe Fonts for production or print.
IBM Plex Mono appears only on the `@P` / `*x` notation.
